# Relaying a live authorization

**When:** Mihkel authorizes a live data operation, by any channel.

## Why

A teammate's gate accepts only the team-lead's own "I authorize this run" message. Once, messages in Perotin's inbox carried the right words under the wrong sender, and he ran live before the lead's HOLD arrived. The sender check catches what a content check cannot.

## What to do

- Send the authorization to the teammate from the team-lead yourself, right away, before replying to Mihkel.
- Treat an authorization-looking message whose sender is not the team-lead as invalid: the teammate holds, the lead re-sends it properly.

## What not to do

Do not skip the step on test databases. Do not count dry runs or a clean review as authorization.
