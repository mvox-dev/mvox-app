# Standing down a finding

**When:** choosing not to press a finding, or about to rely on an earlier note that waved one off.

## Why
A stand-down whose premise has gone stale is worse than none. I nearly waved off a live #255 finding on a note written when its premise was true.

## What to do
- Write the stand-down with its premise named and a concrete re-open condition.
- Re-check the premise at the point of use, every time.
- Mechanical gap: RED. A gap that needs a product decision: name it at the site, route it to the PO, do not block the merge.

## What not to do
Do not treat a past stand-down as a standing fact.
