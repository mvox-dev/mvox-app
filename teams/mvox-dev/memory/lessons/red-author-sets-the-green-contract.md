# RED author sets the GREEN contract

**When:** the team lead is about to tell GREEN what shape to build.

## Why

Proposing the shape straight to GREEN bypassed the RED author; review flagged it for the same reason.

## What to do

- Ask the RED author for a RED that specifies the contract.

## What not to do

- Do not hand GREEN an implementation shape directly.
