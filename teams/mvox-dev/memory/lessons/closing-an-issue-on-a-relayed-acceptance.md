# Closing an issue on a relayed acceptance

**When:** a po-team message says Mihkel accepted an issue.

## Why

The relay is the acceptance; waiting for Mihkel to say it again in the lead session only stalls the board.

## What to do

Close the issue with a closing comment that cites the relay (sender and date).

## What not to do

Do not close without naming the relay; do not ask Mihkel to confirm it again.
