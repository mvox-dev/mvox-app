# Briefing a PO ruling about platform behaviour

**When:** a brief or doc carries a PO ruling that sounds like a statement about Entu's mechanics.

## Why

A PO ruling sets what mvox does, not what the platform does. A brief once carried "editor is fine" as platform fact; Entu's docs put child creation on _expander, which editor includes. The ruling was true as mvox's choice, not as a platform rule.

## What to do

Read the Entu docs (entu/www) first. Write the ruling as mvox's choice on top of the platform rule, citing both.

## What not to do

Do not let a ruling stand in for the platform rule, and do not write "nothing confirms this" without having checked the docs.
