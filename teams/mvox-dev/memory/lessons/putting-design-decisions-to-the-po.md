# Putting design decisions to the PO

**When:** a schema or design session needs several decisions from Mihkel.

## Why

Mihkel: "currently I feel pressed to make multiple decisions concurrently." Batches force early commits.

## What to do

- Ask one decision per turn, with only the context it needs.
- Name what comes next without asking it.
- If sub-decisions hang on a primary one, ask the primary first.
- Record each decision as soon as it is made.

## What not to do

Do not bundle three or four questions, even when they feel grouped.
