# Say what a message supersedes

**When:** sending a brief or correction to an agent in flight.

## Why

Messages crossed in flight: each side acted on the other pre-message state and a sync message was needed.

## What to do

- Lead with one line: "this updates/supersedes message X from time Y".
- Say what is replaced and what is added.

## What not to do

- Do not assume the agent saw your earlier message.
