# A teammate's subagent reports land in the lead session

**When:** writing a brief for a teammate that may spawn its own subagents.

## Why

Hand-back reports from a teammate's subagents go to the lead session, never to the teammate. On
#703 Finn waited for four reports the lead had already received.

## What to do

Tell the teammate that each subagent writes findings to a file under ~/workspace/scratchpad/ and
returns only the path.

## What not to do

Do not let a teammate wait on subagent replies it can never receive.
