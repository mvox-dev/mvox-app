# Respawn an idle teammate with the task in the prompt

**When:** a spawned teammate idles repeatedly with no work product.

## Why

In-process teammates do not reliably act on inbox messages that arrive after spawn; re-sending the brief did not wake one. A fresh spawn with the task in the prompt delivered at once.

## What to do

- Release the idle one and spawn a fresh agent with the task in its spawn prompt.

## What not to do

- Do not keep re-messaging an idle teammate.
